Rules of engagement

The server code

Six rules, enforced without exception. They protect everyone's experience — staff included.

01

Respect between players

No insults, no harassment, no discrimination — in-game and on Discord alike.

02

Zero cheating, zero tolerance

Cheats, exploits or third-party scripts: instant permanent ban, no warning.

03

No stream-sniping or team-stacking

Solo PVP stays solo. Coordinating multiple accounts against one player gets sanctioned.

04

Fighting is the game — outside zones isn't

Combat is the point, but it stays inside the zones built for it.

05

Report bugs, don't exploit them

Found a bug? Open a support ticket. Exploiting it gets you sanctioned instead.

06

Staff decisions are final

Appeals are possible, but only through the dedicated Discord channel.
